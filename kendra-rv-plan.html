Read this first: powering her oxygen & TPN
the one thing that makes or breaks the trip
  • No standard rental Class C has an inverter, so the 120V wall outlets are DEAD while driving unless the onboard generator is running. Plan to run the generator the whole time you're on the road.
  • The generator shuts off at 1/4 tank and must be off while refueling, so there will be short power gaps. Bridge them with a portable lithium power station (1,000 to 2,000Wh, pure sine) for her oxygen concentrator and TPN pump.
  • The RV fridge only cools to about 60°F, too warm for TPN. Keep TPN in a dedicated medical-grade 12V cooler at ~40°F, not the RV fridge.
  • At full-hookup parks you're fine: 30-amp shore power runs the concentrator, pump, and cooler indefinitely. Confirm each rig comes with the right shore cord and adapters.

03

Her gear, sorted

Power, TPN, a lodging backup, and help with the bills.

At the parks: every stop on the plan has full hookups, so 30-amp shore power runs her oxygen concentrator, the TPN pump, and a cooler overnight with no fuss.

While driving: fleet rigs have no inverter, so the outlets only work with the generator running. Run it the whole drive, and lean on the backup lithium power station during fuel stops (the generator and engine must be off to refuel). Carry a full set of spare POC batteries too.

  • Do NOT rely on the RV fridge for TPN. It only reaches about 60°F. Use a dedicated medical-grade 12V cooler at ~40°F, and confirm the exact temperature range with her pharmacy.
  • Carry a physician letter listing every supply (pump, bags, collapsible IV pole, lines, needles, vitamin vials). The Oley Foundation (oley.org) has sample travel letters.
  • Ask the home-infusion pharmacy for enough for the round trip, and have them ship refills to the Houston park if the stay runs long.
  • Keep flushing supplies and a sharps container within easy reach, not buried in storage.

The RV covers her lodging, but if a companion wants a bed off the rig: ACS Hope Lodge Houston (6801 Ardmore St) is free with no income test for a patient in active treatment plus one caregiver. Call 713-848-8905.

MD Anderson's Jesse H. Jones Rotary House offers a discounted medical rate and accessible rooms. Guest relations 713-794-4750.

National: Family Reach (transportation grants during active treatment), Patient Advocate Foundation, and Good Days (travel assistance) can offset fuel and campground costs.

Colorado: Ray of Hope Cancer Foundation (rayofhopecolorado.org) offers gas and travel grants for CO patients. Cancer League of Colorado helps with travel when treatment isn't available locally.

04

Read before you book

The honest fine print, so no one burns a day or a deposit.

  • Nail down the power before anything else. Fleet rigs have no inverter, so plan to run the generator while driving and bring a lithium power station for the gaps. If you can find a peer-to-peer rig with a big inverter and lithium batteries, that's the quietest, safest way to keep her gear running on the move.
  • The RV fridge is too warm for TPN. It only cools to ~60°F. A medical-grade cooler at ~40°F is not optional. Confirm her TPN's temperature range with the pharmacy.
  • Book now, it's peak August. El Monte and the medical-center RV spots both fill up. Reserve the RV and South Main before the rest of the list.
  • Two long driving days. With one overnight, each day is 7 to 8.5 hours. Keep her reclined, stop often, and lean on the buffer days (the 17th and 18th) to recover before the appointment. Vernon, TX is a good alternate overnight if Childress is full.
  • Prices are estimates. RV rates swing with August demand and fees stack (generator, mileage, insurance, kit). Get the all-in quote in writing, and ask peer-to-peer hosts about unlimited mileage.
  • Oxygen and fuel don't mix. Turn the generator and engine off when refueling, and handle her oxygen equipment carefully at fuel stops.
Put together to make the booking easier to split up. Prices and availability shift with the season, so confirm on each call. The move that unlocks the rest: lock the RV (with its power sorted) and the Houston park, then work down the list.
